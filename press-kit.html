SHORT BIO

ARTIST PROFILE

BURDEN GHOST is the alternative rap and metal project founded by Salvadoran artist Jonathan Pérez, born October 3, 1993, in Nueva Guadalupe, El Salvador.

After a period of intense sonic experimentation, the project developed its identity around a raw fusion of metal cadence and instrumentation with rap and hip-hop flows, united by dark and gothic lyricism.

BURDEN GHOST explores isolation, inner struggle, anger, vulnerability, resilience and transformation—turning the feelings people leave unspoken into music.
